# Park the Bus – Privacy Policy

**English** · [فارسی](fa/)

*Effective date: 29 October 2026*

This policy explains what information the mobile game **Park the Bus** (package `com.tpgstudio.parkthebus`, "the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by **TPG Studio**, Türkiye ("we", "us"). Contact: **neshatvarjavand@gmail.com**.

We do not run our own servers for the game, we do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not sell your personal information. The game works through the Google services described below.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, unlocked traps, cosmetics, purchases you made in the game, settings such as language and volume, and which tips you have seen) is saved in a file on your device. It stays on your device unless you sign in to Google Play Games (section 3). Uninstalling the game, or **Settings > Reset progress**, deletes it.

## 2. Ads (Google AdMob) and consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

The game shows two kinds of ads, both provided by **Google AdMob**:

- **Rewarded ads (optional):** you choose to watch one for a reward (an extra life, double coins, a free reroll, an extra daily chest). Nothing is shown unless you tap to watch.
- **Ad breaks (interstitial ads):** a full-screen ad that can appear between levels, only on the way back to the menu or map after a finished level or run, after a short "Ad break" notice. Never during a match, the tutorial levels or the shop, and never when the game starts or closes. They are limited: none in the first 10 minutes of your first session, at most one every 3 minutes of play and every 2 finished levels, none within 3 minutes of a rewarded ad or after a purchase in the same session, and at most 10 a day. The **Remove Ads** purchase turns ad breaks off for good; rewarded ads stay available and optional.

 When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

- your device's **IP address** (which can be used to estimate your general location),
- **device identifiers**: the Android advertising ID and app set ID,
- **interactions** with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps, video views),
- **diagnostic information** (for example app launch time and performance).

This data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses it: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it, and ads are only requested after you have made a choice. You can choose non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy options** (shown where this applies). Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android **Settings > Privacy > Ads**.

## 3. Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to **Google Play Games** (automatically, when you have allowed it for Google Play Games, or from Settings), Google shares your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) with the game, and the game sends to Google, under your Play Games account:

- your **scores** for the Endless and Endless Conveyor leaderboards (a run in which you used a comeback is not sent),
- the **achievements** you unlock,
- a **saved game**: a copy of your progress file (section 1), so it can be restored on another device.

Google also collects analytics and diagnostics about the Play Games software itself. Who can see your profile and scores is set by you in the Play Games app. You can delete your Play Games data for this game from your Play Games profile. Google's terms: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases (gem packs, Remove Ads, the Elite Pass) are processed by **Google Play**. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game that a purchase was made and gives it an order identifier; the game keeps a record of the purchase (product and order identifier) in your progress file so it is delivered exactly once and can be restored (Settings > Restore purchases). If you use Play Games cloud saving, that record is part of the saved game. Google's terms for purchases: [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information ourselves.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
- We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone except the Google services above, which act as described in their own policies.

## 6. Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was sent to the services above, contact us and we will help you delete it.

## 7. Keeping and deleting information

- **On your device:** until you uninstall the game or use Settings > Reset progress.
- **Play Games:** until you delete it from your Play Games profile, or delete your Google account's game data.
- **Ads:** Google keeps ad data according to its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK, Brazil's LGPD, or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data on our own systems, most requests are fulfilled through the controls above (Privacy options, the Android advertising ID settings, the Play Games profile). For anything else, email **neshatvarjavand@gmail.com** and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
